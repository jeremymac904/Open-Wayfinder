# Roadmap

This is a living roadmap, not a promise of delivery dates.

## Available in this repository

- Copyable personal coach persona and privacy guidance.
- Starter onboarding, daily check-in, weekly review and business assistant builder.
- Private profile, check-in and business assistant templates.
- A quick-start guide and contribution path.

These are early instructions and templates. They have not yet been validated across multiple hosts.

## In development / planned

- Tested platform-specific setup guides and an evidence-backed capability matrix.
- A maintained review of platform tools, connectors, skills, MCP, file access and scheduling.
- Fitness logs, recovery, nutrition, goals, finance and other coaching skills.
- Isa and Noah as optional coaches, once source assets and distribution rights are documented.
- Private dashboards, pattern insights, a personal playbook and future-self reflection.
- Exercise demonstrations with technique review, and machine-coaching guidance.
- Expanded assistant templates, example workflows and optional platform integrations.
- Walkthrough videos and a more complete contributor experience.

A scheduled review can research platform changes; it does not make an integration work or guarantee a platform remains unchanged. See issues for current work.
