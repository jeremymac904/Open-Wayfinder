## What is Open Wayfinder?

Open Wayfinder gives you a starting point for creating an AI coach and assistants using tools you already have. Work on your health, goals, relationships, creativity and career ... or build assistants to help with marketing, operations, sales and research.

You choose your platform, your goals and what you share. Your coach should work with you, remember what matters when your platform can access it, and help you take the next useful step.

> **Early development:** The starter instructions and templates in this repository are available now. The skills and platform guides are initial drafts. Automated syncing, working integrations, reminders, dashboards, reviewed demo videos and platform-specific end-to-end validation are still in progress.

## Get started in two steps

1. Open [Get started](docs/getting-started.md), choose your AI platform, and copy the coach instructions into a new conversation or its custom-instructions area.
2. Tell your coach: **“Help me set up Open Wayfinder. Start with one question.”**

Want a business assistant? Copy the [assistant builder instructions](skills/business-assistant-builder/SKILL.md) into a conversation and describe what you do.

No installation, payment, account with this project, or technical setup is required to read and copy the starter files. Your chosen AI platform may have its own account, pricing, storage, and feature requirements.

## Make it yours

- **A coach for real life:** Work through goals, habits, training, relationships, career and creativity at a pace that fits you.
- **An AI team for your work:** Define an assistant's job, boundaries, tools and approval rules, then adapt it to your platform.
- **Your information, your choice:** Keep a private profile, decide what to share, and export or edit it yourself.
- **Honest capabilities:** A prompt cannot magically connect accounts, read files, schedule reminders or send messages. Those depend on your host and the permissions you grant.

## Privacy and safety

This repository is public. **Never upload your personal profile, health details, private business information, passwords or client records here.** Read the [privacy guide](docs/privacy-and-safety.md) before sharing information with an AI service.

Open Wayfinder is designed for adults. It provides coaching and educational information, not medical, mental-health, legal or financial services. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S., call or text **988** for mental-health crisis support.
