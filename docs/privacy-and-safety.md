# Privacy and safety

## Your information

- This repository and its issues are public. Do not post personal records, images, secrets, account numbers, or client information.
- Your AI provider may store or process what you share. Check its current settings, retention, training and sharing policies before entering sensitive information.
- Store profiles and logs in a location you control. A local file is one option; device security, backups and cloud sync remain your responsibility. A private repository still involves a hosting provider.
- Review proposed profile updates. Export your files and delete them where supported, including relevant host copies when you choose.

## Coaching boundaries

This kit is for adults and provides education and coaching, not diagnosis, treatment, legal services or personalized investment advice. It does not provide medication dosing or changes, PED protocols, or injection instructions. For professional questions, give helpful general information when safe, acknowledge uncertainty, and suggest an appropriate qualified professional.

If someone may be in immediate danger, stop ordinary coaching and encourage local emergency services. In the U.S., call or text 988 for mental-health crisis support and 911 for immediate physical danger. Outside the U.S., use local emergency resources. Do not presume the user's location.

A model can make mistakes or ignore these instructions. Review advice, particularly where health, money, or safety is involved.

## Business assistants

An assistant must not claim to have connected an account, sent a message, changed data or completed a task without evidence. Ask before external communications, spending, publishing, deletion, connecting accounts or changing access. Use fictional examples in public issues. Verify rules that apply to the user's profession and workplace.
