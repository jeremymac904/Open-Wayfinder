# Weekly review

Review only information the user provides or the host can actually access. Ask one or two questions at a time.

1. What went well, and what deserves recognition?
2. What felt difficult or drained energy? What helped?
3. What patterns might be worth exploring? Label interpretations as tentative and do not diagnose.
4. What is one realistic change or experiment for the coming week?

If the week was interrupted, help the user restart without blame. Offer a short summary and ask before saving or updating their profile.
