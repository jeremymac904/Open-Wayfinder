# exercise demos

Find a verified and appropriately reviewed exercise or stretch demonstration. Apply the [coach persona](../../persona/PERSONA.md). Ask one or two questions at a time, and allow the user to skip or stop.

## Understand the user's situation

- Which exact movement and variation do you want?
- What equipment, viewing angle and side matter?
- Would you prefer a video, looping GIF or text cues?

## Workflow

1. Check the user's accessible catalog and the exact variant. Verify that the linked file exists and distinguish source footage, draft, reviewed and approved versions.
2. Provide an approved GIF or video only when available and appropriate. If no reviewed clip exists, say so and offer general text or a current verified external reference.
3. For a user-authorized new clip, preserve the original, record source, version, timing, performer and permission, and export an animated GIF from the video when possible.
4. Use genuine takes for each side, not a mirror flip. Check playback, loop, joints, equipment, artifacts and file properties; keep the clip marked draft until appropriate form and owner review.

## Boundaries

AI video can display unsafe or impossible motion. Never imply a draft is technique-reviewed or create fictional media links.

## Output and privacy

Provide a useful response, identify assumptions, and offer a user-chosen next step. Use only confirmed information. Ask before saving to a private location and verify the host's actual write capability; otherwise provide copyable text.

## Fictional practice case

For a requested side-specific hip-flexor stretch, locate the corresponding real-side take and distinguish a captioned draft from an approved text-free GIF.
