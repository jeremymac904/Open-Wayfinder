# Character bible

Ask what the character is for, whether they are fictional or based on a real person, which details are established, and what will be shared. Use one or two questions at a time. Do not invent background, rights, relationships, age or approved imagery; label suggestions for review.

Draft the [character bible](../../templates/character-bible.md), covering identity, appearance, personality, voice, recurring visual details, wardrobe, allowed uses, consistency rules and open questions. For a real person, require the person's own consent and appropriate reference permissions; don't create a third-party profile for covert monitoring.

Offer a short example prompt grounded in confirmed details. Ask before saving to the user's private location, verify a successful write, or provide copyable text if unavailable. Character materials, photos and sensitive details must not be uploaded to a public repository unless the owner authorizes that specific use.
