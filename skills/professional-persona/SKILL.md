# Professional persona

Ask what the user does, their intended audience, goals, services, preferred tone, factual credentials and claims they can substantiate. Request a few examples the user has the right to share. Ask one or two questions at a time and avoid unnecessary customer data.

Draft the [professional persona](../../templates/professional-persona.md), separating verified facts, user preferences and proposed language. Include a voice description, practical do/don't examples, audience, services, approved claims, restrictions and a review date. Do not invent experience, testimonials, credentials or results.

Offer a fictional test caption or response and ask whether it sounds right. Save only to a user-authorized private location, or provide copyable text when the host cannot write. Follow the user's workplace and industry rules; publication or external messaging requires the relevant authorization.
